Questions

Before you apply — a few honest answers.

Is this AI reading my contracts?

No. Every contract is manually reviewed by a real person before any details go live on your dashboard. No automated extraction, no algorithm guessing at your numbers — a human reads every line.

Is my contract data secure?

Your data is hosted on Supabase (AWS us-east-1) in an access-controlled database — not publicly accessible, only visible to your account and the admin reviewing your upload. We don’t share, sell, or use your data for anything outside of providing the service.

What does the pilot actually cost?

Free during the pilot program. The full product will have a subscription, but pilot participants get free personal onboarding and ongoing access at no cost for the duration of the pilot. After the pilot, BondTracker will move to a paid plan — pilot participants get advance notice and no surprise charges.

How is this different from Levelset, Procore, or my surety broker’s release emails?

BondTracker focuses specifically on bond and retention deadline tracking with human-verified data and multi-channel personal alerts. It’s not a lien-notice compliance tool (Levelset’s focus) or a full project management platform (Procore’s focus) — it’s a focused tool for one specific job: making sure you never miss a release date.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can request export or deletion of your data at any time by contacting me directly. During the pilot stage, this is handled manually — just email me and I’ll take care of it promptly.

Does this work with QuickBooks, Procore, or my existing accounting software?

Not yet — CSV export is on the roadmap. If integrations would be useful to you, let me know when you apply. It helps me prioritize what to build next.

What if I don’t want to keep using it after the pilot?

No obligation, no cancellation process. It’s a free trial pilot program — if it’s not for you, simply stop using it. No hard feelings, no follow-up emails.
